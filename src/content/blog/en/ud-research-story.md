A University of Delaware story by Hilary Douwes about CosmicWatch ran on [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260917003725.htm) on September 17, 2026. It covers how the detector started as a teaching tool and is now used in research as well, including for calibration at UD's NuDot experiment and the Coherent CAPTAIN-Mills detector at Los Alamos.

A week later, [Electronics For You](https://www.electronicsforu.com/news/raspberry-pi-pico-detector-catches-cosmic-ray-muons-for-under-100) wrote up the v3X hardware for its readers, with a note on how schools and college labs could use it.
