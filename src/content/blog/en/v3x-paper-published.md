*CosmicWatch: The Desktop Muon Detector (v3X)*, by Spencer Axani, Masooma Sarfraz, Miles Garcia, Collin Owens, Katarzyna Frankiewicz and Janet M. Conrad, appeared in the Journal of Instrumentation, volume 20, in October 2025.

It covers the new analog electronics, the move to the Raspberry Pi Pico, coincidence logic that cuts accidental coincidences by almost two orders of magnitude, and four example measurements, including a balloon flight to 31 km.

- Journal: [doi:10.1088/1748-0221/20/10/P10040](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-0221/20/10/P10040)
- Free copy: [arXiv:2508.12111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12111)
