**Watch for a dip in the rate after a big solar eruption.**

## What you need

- A detector pair logging continuously for weeks
- Pressure data to correct the rate

## How it was done

After a large coronal mass ejection, the magnetic field it carries can sweep cosmic rays away from Earth for a few days. Ground detectors see the rate drop by a few percent, then recover. This is called a Forbush decrease.

Log continuously, correct for pressure, and compare against solar activity reports. Several detectors or several sites make the dip much easier to see.
