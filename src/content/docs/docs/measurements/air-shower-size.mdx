**Separate two detectors and see how coincidences fall with distance.**

## What you need

- Two detectors with a long coincidence cable, or accurate time stamps

## How it was done

One high-energy cosmic ray can rain particles over a wide area. Two detectors far apart only fire together when they are both inside the same shower. Measure the coincidence rate as you move them apart and you are measuring how big showers are.

With cheap GPS timing, which the lab is working on, detectors could be spread across a town or a state and still be matched event by event.
