**Fly a pair to the stratosphere and find the Regener–Pfotzer maximum.**

## What you need

- Two detectors and a 5,000 mAh battery
- A high-altitude balloon payload

## How it was done

Two v3X detectors flew on a high-altitude balloon to about 31 km, powered by one 5,000 mAh battery and logging to microSD. The onboard pressure sensor gives the altitude through the standard barometric formula, so no GPS is needed for the altitude axis.

The count rate rises with altitude to a broad peak near 20 km, the Regener–Pfotzer maximum, where new particles are made as fast as they are absorbed. Above it the rate falls again. At the peak, about 52 Hz, dead time stayed under 2%.

The accelerometer and gyroscope tell the rest of the story: the payload launched on its side, righted itself, and spun around the vertical for most of the flight.
