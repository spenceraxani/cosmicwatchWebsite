**Use the pulse-height spectrum to look at gamma sources.**

## What you need

- One detector
- A check source or a naturally radioactive object

## How it was done

The detector measures the height of every pulse, which is related to the energy each particle leaves in the scintillator. Put a gamma source near the detector and the pulse-height spectrum changes shape.

Plastic scintillator mostly records gamma rays through Compton scattering, so you will see edges rather than sharp peaks. Compare spectra with and without the source, and measure how the rate falls off with distance to see the inverse-square law.
