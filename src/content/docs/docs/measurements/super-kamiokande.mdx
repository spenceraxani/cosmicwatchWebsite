**How few muons reach a kilometer under a mountain.**

## What you need

- A detector pair
- A very deep laboratory

## How it was done

Super-Kamiokande, the neutrino detector in Japan, sits about 1 km under Mount Ikeno so that the rock blocks almost all cosmic-ray muons. A CosmicWatch detector taken inside showed a dramatic drop in rate compared with the surface, which is exactly why neutrino experiments go deep underground.
