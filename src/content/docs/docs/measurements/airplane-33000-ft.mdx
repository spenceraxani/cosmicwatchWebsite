**Bring a detector on a commercial flight.**

## What you need

- One detector and a power bank
- A window seat helps with photos, not with data

## How it was done

A detector in a carry-on bag is enough to see the rate rise as the plane climbs and settle at cruising altitude, far above the rate on the ground.

Log to microSD, note your takeoff and landing times, and match the rate to the flight's altitude profile from a flight-tracking site afterward.
