The first step is to purchase the required components. This page lists everything we use to build one v3X detector. Most of the electronics come from Digi-Key; the rest (scintillator, OLED, sensor boards, cables, screws and the case) come from the stores listed in the tables. If you are building a single detector, we still recommend purchasing extras of the small surface-mount resistors and capacitors.

**Cost:** about {totals.required} in required parts for one detector, plus {totals.optional} for the aluminum case and optional extras. {totals.digikeyRequired} of the {totals.requiredCount} required parts can be ordered from Digi-Key in one go.

**Source:** the lab's purchasing spreadsheet on GitHub. Prices are single-unit prices and will drift; some parts are only sold in bulk.

## Ordering from Digi-Key, step by step

We tested this list in a real Digi-Key cart on November 8, 2026: all 33 Digi-Key lines resolve to the right parts. You can check out as a guest; no account is needed.

<Steps>

1. **Copy the list.** Pick the tab for the number of detectors you are building, then click the copy button in the top corner of the list.

   <Screenshot src={step1} alt="The Digi-Key order list on this page, set to one detector, with the copy button in its top corner" />

2. **Open the Digi-Key cart.** Go to [digikey.com/ordering/shoppingcart](https://www.digikey.com/ordering/shoppingcart). Below the cart is a **Bulk Add** box.

   <Screenshot src={step2} alt="The empty Digi-Key cart with the Bulk Add box below it" />

3. **Paste and add.** Paste the list into **Bulk Add** and click the **Add to Cart** button next to it.

   <Screenshot src={step3} alt="The 33-line parts list pasted into Bulk Add, with the Add to Cart button next to it" />

4. **Confirm the held lines.** For one detector, 29 parts go straight into the cart and 4 are held back with the message "A lower unit price is available at a higher quantity". This is not an error.

   <Screenshot src={step4} alt="Four lines held back below the cart, each with a View Options link" />

   Click **View Options** on each one. Digi-Key's Value Calculator opens with the quantity from the list already selected. Click **Add** to keep it, or pick the larger quantity first: for the 1k resistor, 10 cost $0.38 while 8 cost $0.80.

   <Screenshot src={step4calc} alt="Digi-Key's Value Calculator for the 1k resistor, offering 8, 10 or 25 pieces" />

5. **Check the cart.** You should have 33 items, a $47.37 subtotal for one detector before shipping and tariff.

   <Screenshot src={step5} alt="The Digi-Key cart with 33 items and a $47.37 subtotal" />

   Then check the **Availability** column. A backordered part shows the date Digi-Key expects stock, with links to an alternate package or substitutes.

   <Screenshot src={step5back} alt="A backordered 4.7 µF capacitor in the cart, expected in stock on November 18, 2026, with View Substitutes" />

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Backorders when we tested (November 8, 2026)">
Four parts were on backorder: the 4.7 µF capacitor (490-10751-1-ND), the 1 µF capacitor (1276-2928-1-ND), the 6-pin socket (1212-1229-ND) and the BAT54S diode (4878-BAT54SCT-ND). Order early, or use **View Substitutes** to pick an equivalent part that is in stock.
</Aside>

The rest of the parts (scintillator, optical gel, OLED, BMP280 and motion-sensor boards, buzzer, LEDs, microSD socket and card, cables, standoffs and screws, tape and foil, and the case) are not sold by Digi-Key; their stores are listed in the tables above. There are several companies that sell plastic scintillator, but we found that physics departments often have leftover scintillator from previous experiments.
