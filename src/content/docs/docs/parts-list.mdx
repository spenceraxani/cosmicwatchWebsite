The first step to build a detector is to purchase the required components. Most of the electronics come from Digi-Key, while the rest (scintillator, OLED, sensor boards, cables, screws and the case) come from the stores listed in the tables. If you are building a single detector, we recommend purchasing extras of the small surface-mount resistors and capacitors. The cost is about {totals.required} in required parts for one detector, and {totals.optional} for the aluminum case and optional extras.

## Digi-Key Order List

Copy the list for the number of detectors you are building and paste it into **Bulk Add** in the Digi-Key cart. Each line is `quantity, Digi-Key part number`. The step-by-step guide is [below the tables](#how-to-order-from-digi-key).

## How to Order From Digi-Key

We strongly suggest ordering more than you need for the number of detectors you are building. Small parts are much cheaper per piece in larger quantities, and spares save a second order if a part is lost or damaged while soldering.

<Steps>

1. **Copy the list.** Pick the tab for the number of detectors you are building, then click the copy button in the top corner of the list.

   <Screenshot
     src={step1}
     alt="The Digi-Key order list on this page, set to one detector, with the copy button in its top corner"
   />

2. **Open the Digi-Key cart.** Go to [digikey.com/ordering/shoppingcart](https://www.digikey.com/ordering/shoppingcart). Below the cart is a **Bulk Add** box.

   <Screenshot
     src={step2}
     alt="The empty Digi-Key cart with the Bulk Add box below it"
   />

3. **Paste and add.** Paste the list into **Bulk Add** and click the **Add to Cart** button next to it.

   <Screenshot
     src={step3}
     alt="The 33-line parts list pasted into Bulk Add, with the Add to Cart button next to it"
   />

4. **Confirm the held lines.** For one detector, 29 parts go straight into the cart and 4 are held back with the message "A lower unit price is available at a higher quantity". This is not an error.

   <Screenshot
     src={step4}
     alt="Four lines held back below the cart, each with a View Options link"
   />

   Click **View Options** on each. Digi-Key's Value Calculator opens with the quantity from the list already selected. Click **Add** to keep it, or pick the larger quantity first (for the 1k resistor, 10 cost about $0.38 while 8 cost about $0.80).

   <Screenshot
     src={step4calc}
     alt="Digi-Key's Value Calculator for the 1k resistor, offering 8, 10 or 25 pieces"
   />

5. **Check the cart.** You should have 33 items, with around a $47.37 subtotal for one detector before shipping and tariff.

   <Screenshot
     src={step5}
     alt="The Digi-Key cart with 33 items and a $47.37 subtotal"
   />

   Then check the **Availability** column. Some parts may be backordered, like the four that were backordered in our screenshots:

   <Aside type="caution" title="Backordered when we ordered (November 8, 2026)">
     The 4.7 µF capacitor (490-10751-1-ND), the 1 µF capacitor (1276-2928-1-ND),
     the 6-pin socket (1212-1229-ND) and the BAT54S diode (4878-BAT54SCT-ND).
     Order early, or use **View Substitutes** to pick an equivalent part that is
     in stock.
   </Aside>

   <Screenshot
     src={step5back}
     alt="A backordered 4.7 µF capacitor in the cart, expected in stock on November 18, 2026, with View Substitutes"
   />

</Steps>

The rest of the parts (scintillator, optical gel, OLED, BMP280 and motion-sensor boards, buzzer, LEDs, microSD socket and card, cables, standoffs and screws, tape and foil, and the case) are not sold by Digi-Key. Their stores are listed in the tables above, or in the BOM that you can download.
